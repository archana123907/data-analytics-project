# Zomato Delivery Dashboard

## Project Overview

The **Zomato Delivery Dashboard** is an interactive Excel-based data analytics project designed to analyze and visualize food delivery data. The dashboard transforms raw order-level data into meaningful business insights related to sales, customer ratings, delivery performance, cancellations, payment methods, cuisines, cities, and delivery partners.

The project uses **Microsoft Excel** to perform data analysis, create Key Performance Indicators (KPIs), build charts, and provide interactive filtering options. The main objective is to make large amounts of food delivery data easier to understand and help identify important trends and patterns.

## Key Features

The dashboard contains several KPI cards that provide a quick overview of business performance:

* **Total Orders** – Number of orders received.
* **Total Revenue** – Overall revenue generated from orders.
* **Average Rating** – Average customer rating.
* **Average Delivery Time** – Average time taken to deliver orders.
* **Cancellation Percentage** – Percentage of orders that were cancelled.

## Data Visualizations

The dashboard includes multiple visualizations to analyze different aspects of the business:

* **Revenue by Month** – Shows monthly revenue trends and helps identify changes in sales over time.
* **Revenue by City** – Compares revenue generated across different cities.
* **Cuisine Sales** – Shows the sales contribution of different cuisine categories.
* **Delivery Partner Performance** – Compares delivery partners based on order and delivery performance.
* **Payment Mode Analysis** – Shows the distribution of different payment methods used by customers.
* **Ratings Analysis** – Displays the distribution of customer ratings.
* **Peak Hour Orders** – Identifies the hours during which order volumes are highest.
* **Weekday Orders** – Analyzes order patterns across different days of the week.

## Interactive Filters

The dashboard provides interactive filters for:

* City
* Restaurant
* Cuisine
* Payment Mode
* Delivery Partner
* Month

These filters allow users to analyze specific segments of the data and explore the dashboard dynamically.

## Tools & Technologies Used

* **Microsoft Excel**

## Project Objective

The primary objective of this project is to convert raw Zomato delivery data into an easy-to-use analytical dashboard. It helps users quickly understand **sales performance, customer behavior, delivery efficiency, cancellation trends, and operational patterns** through interactive visualizations.

## Business Insights

The dashboard can help businesses identify:

* Monthly revenue trends.
* High-performing and low-performing cities.
* Popular cuisine categories.
* Customer rating patterns.
* Peak ordering hours.
* Weekday ordering trends.
* Frequently used payment methods.
* Delivery partner performance.
* Cancellation patterns.

Overall, this project demonstrates the use of **Excel for data analysis, business intelligence, and dashboard development**, while presenting complex data in a simple and visually understandable format.
